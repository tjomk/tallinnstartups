
To create the Open Graph image manually:
1. Create a 1200x630 px image
2. Use a gradient background (blue to light blue)
3. Add text: "Estonian Startup Jobs"
4. Add subtitle: "Find your dream job at Estonia's most innovative startups"
5. Save as og-default.png in this directory

Recommended tools:
- Canva (canva.com)
- Figma (figma.com) 
- Adobe Photoshop/Illustrator
- GIMP (free alternative)

Colors to use:
- Primary blue: #1980e6
- Light blue: #60a5fa
- White text: #ffffff
- Dark text: #1f2937
